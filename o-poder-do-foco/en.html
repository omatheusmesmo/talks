The point

Two paths that look nothing alike.

Different projects, different communities. Same destination.

30 seconds. Just the bridge.
The thesis

Focus.

It is not the volume of what you do. It is repetition in one place, in the code and in the community, until people can attach your name to something.

One sentence. The evidence comes in the conversation.
Where focus happens

Code is half. Community is the other.

In the code

  • Issues and triage
  • Review and documentation
  • Pull requests

In the community

  • The same meetup, every month
  • CFPs, talks, events
  • Mentoring and organizing

People who only write code get discovered by accident. People who show up get remembered.

The slide that balances the talk. Tell one quick story of an opportunity that came from an event, not from a PR. Events are where referrals are born.
