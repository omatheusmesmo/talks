Reason 01 · Learn from the maintainers

What I thought contributing was.

What I thought

  • Writing code that works
  • Sending the PR and waiting for the merge
  • A review is a judgment

What the review taught me

  • To think about the design of the solution, not just the line
  • To spot the mistake I couldn't see alone
  • To write better Java with the people who maintain the project

The maintainers didn't just review. They taught me.
